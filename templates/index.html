Supported Diagnostic Classes

The system strictly isolates kidney pathology into one of four clinically standardized categories. No extraneous or unverified classifications are introduced.

Category 1

NORMAL

Intact renal parenchyma, sharp cortical contours, symmetrical corticomedullary distinction, and no abnormal calcification or masses.

Category 2

STONE

Renal calculi or nephrolithiasis showing focal hyperdense calcification attenuation within the renal pelvis, calyces, or ureter.

Category 3

CYST

Simple or complex fluid-filled lesions exhibiting characteristic low attenuation (near-water density), imperceptible walls, and no contrast enhancement.

Category 4

TUMOR

Solid renal cell masses characterized by architectural distortion, irregular margins, heterogeneity, and distinct soft-tissue enhancement.

Dual-Classifier Machine Learning Architecture

Unlike opaque neural networks that obscure their decision boundaries, NephroScan AI utilizes reproducible radiomic feature extraction coupled with an ensemble of Support Vector Machines and Decision Trees.

1
Standardized Radiomic Feature Extraction

96-dimensional vector capturing Hounsfield-analogous intensity histograms, texture gradients, spatial symmetry quadrants, and edge distributions.

2
Dual Model Consensus

Both the RBF-kernel SVM and the Decision Tree generate independent probability distributions. Predictions are reconciled via deterministic ensemble rules.

3
Calibrated Uncertainty Safeguard

Scans failing to meet minimum confidence thresholds trigger an automated clinical warning rather than delivering spurious diagnoses.
